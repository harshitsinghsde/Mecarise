How an Engagement Runs

A fixed sequence, not a black box.

Every audit follows the same four stages regardless of discipline, so you always know what's next and what it depends on.

01

Document & data intake

We request twelve months of utility bills, single-line diagrams, load lists and maintenance records ahead of the visit, so site time is spent measuring, not searching for paperwork.

TYPICAL DURATION · 3–5 DAYS
02

Site measurement

Certified auditors run load logging, thermal scans and power quality capture on live equipment, cross-checked against your operating schedule.

TYPICAL DURATION · 1–10 DAYS, BY FACILITY SIZE
03

Analysis & findings review

Data is modelled against baseline consumption to rank findings by payback period and safety risk before anything is finalised.

TYPICAL DURATION · 5–7 DAYS
04

Report & implementation support

You receive a prioritised action list with cost, savings and compliance impact for each item, plus support through implementation and verification.
